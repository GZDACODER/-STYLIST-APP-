# Industry research for Clientele's business-type templates

Researched November 23, 2026 from public sources only: vendor feature and pricing
pages, help articles, app-store listings, and reviews on Capterra, G2, GetApp,
Trustpilot and similar sites. Nothing was logged into or signed up for. Everything
here is summarised in our own words.

**How much to trust it.** Many of the "best X software" comparisons are written by
one of the vendors, or are affiliate pages. Prices change often and sources disagree
(noted where they do). Treat prices as approximate and check them on the vendor's own
pricing page before quoting them. Points marked *(our view)* are Clientele's own
judgement, not something a source said.

Clientele today: $25/month all-in, plus a 3% + 30¢ fee on deposits only (it covers
Stripe's card fee). Clients book without making an account. Included: Regulars-only
times, a waitlist, deposits, standing appointments, intake forms and a waiver,
aftercare emails, My look, a client home with Pro ID sign-in, an Assistant add-on
($10/month), and templates for Hair stylist, Barber, Tattoo artist and Tutor.

## 1. Barber

**The tools:** Squire (shop software), Booksy (booking plus a marketplace), theCut
(a barber-first app with a client marketplace), Square Appointments, Fresha, Setmore,
GlossGenius, Vagaro, plus newer barber-only apps (Barbr, BookFade, MyCuts, hey.booked).

1. **Must-haves:** online booking from a link or Instagram; text reminders;
   no-show/cancellation fee protection (card on file or deposit); taking payments
   and tips; client notes (how they wear it); rebooking; a calendar for several
   chairs in a shop.
2. **Dashboard:** today's chair list in time order; who's next or who's in the
   chair; today's money and tips; open slots. Main tabs: Calendar, Clients,
   Checkout/Payments, Reports, Marketing.
3. **Booking rules:** short fixed lengths (15-60 min); lots of walk-ins (some
   tools have a digital walk-in queue or waitlist); rebooking every 2-3 weeks
   is the habit; the deposit is usually small or replaced by a card on file
   with a no-show fee.
4. **Intake:** light. Usual cut and guard numbers, skin sensitivity, photo consent.
   Few waivers apart from chemical services (relaxers, color).
5. **Client page:** pick the barber, then the service and a time; see past cuts;
   rebook; tip after paying.
6. **Money:** tips (on the card or cash) are central; no-show fees; booth/chair
   rent for shop owners (Squire collects rent on its higher tiers); some apps
   charge the client a booking fee.
7. **Top complaints:**
   - Fees that grow: per-barber pricing (Booksy +$20 per barber); client booking
     fees (theCut ~$0.95, Squire $1-3); marketplace commissions on new clients
     (Booksy Boost ~30% of a first visit).
   - Slow mobile calendars.
   - Deposits that are hard to set up.
   - No way to block a client who never paid their no-show fee.
   - Weak support.
8. **Pricing:**
   - Squire: about $30/month solo, up to $150-250 for shops.
   - Booksy: $29.99/month + $20 per extra barber.
   - theCut: free tier, Pro about $25 (sources conflict), plus client fees.
   - Square: free, or $49/$149 per location, plus card fees.
   - **What Clientele beats:** the same $25 flat price as the cheapest paid tiers,
     with no client booking fee, no marketplace commission and no per-feature
     add-ons.
9. **Wow features we could offer:**
   - A "Next in chair" walk-in queue: clients join from a QR code in the shop and
     get a text when it's nearly their turn.
   - One-tap "same cut, same time in 2 weeks" rebooking from the after-visit
     email and the client home.
   - Block anyone with an unpaid no-show fee until they've paid (a known gap in
     theCut).
   - My look photos of each cut, shown to the client before their next visit
     ("same as last time?").
   - Regulars-first slots for loyal clients (Clientele already has these; barbers
     rarely get them).
10. **Their words:** clients; cuts; appointments or "slots"; walk-ins; chair;
    lineup, fade, taper; tips; book a cut.

**Recommended Barber template:**
- **Home cards:** Today (chair list) · Walk-in queue · Today's money · Rebook rate ·
  New clients.
- **Tabs:** Today, Calendar, Clients, Openings, Waitlist (renamed "Walk-ins"),
  Notes, Settings.
- **Intake:** usual cut / guard numbers; skin sensitivity / razor bumps; photo consent.
- **Services:** Skin Fade 45 min $35; Lineup 15 min $15; Beard Trim 20 min $20;
  Kids' Cut 30 min $25; Cut + Beard 60 min $50.
- **Rules:** small deposits ($5) or none; a same-day waitlist; "rebook in 2 weeks"
  after each visit.
- **Follow-ups:** a "time for a fresh cut" nudge after 2-3 weeks.
- **Show:** My look, Regulars, Waitlist, Book my usual, standing appointments.
  **Hide:** aftercare tips by default.

**Pitch:** "Flat $25, no fees on your clients and no cut of your new ones. Plus a
walk-in queue and one-tap rebooking that keep your chair full."

## 2. Tattoo artist

**The tools:** Porter (tattoo-only), Vagaro, Venue Ink, Tattoo Studio Pro, Linework,
TattooGenda, Square Appointments, and many artists still on Instagram DMs plus
JotForm or Tally forms.

1. **Must-haves:** a booking request form (idea, placement, size, reference
   photos) that the artist approves before the client can book; consultations
   kept separate from sessions; a deposit at booking (non-refundable, counted
   toward the final price); a digital consent form or waiver signed before the
   appointment, with an ID upload; multi-session projects.
2. **Dashboard:** a requests inbox to review ("Your Turn" queue); upcoming
   sessions; deposits collected; projects in progress.
3. **Booking rules:** request, then approve, then the client picks a time and pays
   the deposit. Sessions are long (2-8 hours) and often several per piece. The
   deposit is held until the last session. Deposits are lost for late arrival,
   no-shows, or arriving without ID.
4. **Intake:**
   - Questions: date of birth and 18+ with photo ID (the minimum age varies by
     state, and some states allow 16-17 with notarised parental consent); health
     questions (medications, blood thinners, skin conditions, pregnancy,
     allergies); not under the influence of alcohol or drugs; acknowledging the
     tattoo is permanent and healing is the client's responsibility.
   - Agreements: photo/portfolio release; custom designs remain the artist's
     property.
5. **Client page:** a request form with photo uploads; a link to sign documents
   and upload ID on the day; aftercare instructions afterwards.
6. **Money:** deposits ($50-200 typical, non-refundable, applied to the final
   price); tips; payment after each session or at the end of a project; shop
   commission splits (shop tiers).
7. **Top complaints:**
   - Forms are often a paid add-on (Vagaro lists Forms at $10/month extra).
   - The consent record isn't stored properly (an issue raised about Venue).
   - DM back-and-forth chaos.
   - Clients who ghost after the consultation.
8. **Pricing:**
   - Porter: about $35/month for an artist, about $200 for a shop.
   - Vagaro: about $24-30, plus add-ons.
   - Tattoo Studio Pro: about $29.
   - **What Clientele beats:** $25 all-in, with forms, waivers, deposits and
     aftercare included.
9. **Wow features we could offer:**
   - A request inbox with one-tap "Approve and send booking link", which can
     require a consultation first.
   - Projects: one piece made of several sessions, with deposit and balance
     tracked across all of them.
   - A signed consent plus ID kept as a PDF on the client card.
   - Healing check-ins at day 1, 3 and 14, with a "send a healing photo" link.
   - Touch-up booking within 60 days.
10. **Their words:** clients; consultation (consult); session; piece; project;
    flash; custom; deposit; placement; touch-up; healing; book a session.

**Recommended Tattoo template:**
- **Home cards:** Requests to review · Today's sessions · Deposits held ·
  Projects in progress · Touch-ups due.
- **Tabs:** Today, Requests, Calendar, Clients, Projects, Notes, Settings.
- **Intake:** date of birth + 18+ + ID upload; placement; size; idea + reference
  photos; health (medications, blood thinners, skin, pregnancy, allergies); not
  under the influence; plus the consent waiver below.
- **Services:** Consultation 30 min free; Small (up to 3") 90 min; Half day 4 h;
  Full day 7 h; Flash (fixed price); Touch-up 30 min.
- **Rules:** a deposit is required on every session and goes toward the final
  price; the request must be approved before booking; multi-session projects.
- **Follow-ups:** healing emails at day 1, 3 and 14; a touch-up reminder.
- **Hide:** Book my usual and standing appointments. **Show:** waiver, projects,
  My look (as a "healed photo").

**Pitch:** "Requests, consults, deposits, consent and ID, all in one link. $25
flat, with forms included, not sold as an add-on."

## 3. Tutor / tutoring business

**The tools:** TutorBird, Teachworks, TutorCruncher, Teach 'n Go, Oases Online
(larger centres), Tutorbase, Wise, plus general schedulers (Calendly, Acuity).

1. **Must-haves:**
   - **Parents:** family accounts (parent and student linked); a parent/student
     portal.
   - **Lessons:** recurring weekly lessons; online lesson links (Zoom, Meet) or
     a place.
   - **Records:** attendance; lesson notes.
   - **Money:** invoicing with auto-pay; lesson packages and credits; makeup
     credits for missed lessons.
2. **Dashboard:** today's lessons; attendance to take; unpaid invoices; package
   balances running low; reports on hours and revenue.
3. **Booking rules:** a fixed weekly slot per student; term or semester
   schedules; packages of 5-20 lessons; a cancellation window (24-48 h) with
   makeup credit; a free assessment first.
4. **Intake:** student's grade and subject; goals; parent/guardian contact and
   consent for minors; online or in person; learning needs.
5. **Client page:** a parent portal with upcoming lessons, notes and progress,
   invoices and pay, and a booking or rescheduling request.
6. **Money:**
   - packages, prepaid or monthly
   - auto-pay
   - late fees
   - makeup credits
   - tutor pay and payroll, for centres
7. **Top complaints:**
   - Applying packages to lessons is confusing (Teachworks reviewers).
   - Clients couldn't buy packages themselves (TutorCruncher).
   - Too many settings to learn ("make-up credit" is jargon).
   - Per-lesson pricing climbs as you grow.
   - In tutoring services generally: sessions changed without telling the
     parent, and unclear cancellation refunds.
8. **Pricing:**
   - TutorBird: about $15-17/month + about $5 per extra tutor.
   - Teachworks: $16-188/month plus a per-lesson fee.
   - Oases: about $699/month for large centres.
   - **What Clientele beats:** cheaper tools exist (TutorBird), but none is flat
     with no per-lesson fees and also includes a polished booking page,
     reminders and a waitlist. Our edge is design and simplicity *(our view)*.
9. **Wow features we could offer:**
   - Parent updates: after each session, a short progress note emailed to the
     parent automatically, with "what to practise".
   - Packages that count themselves down: a "3 sessions left, renew?" link.
   - Online sessions: the meeting link sits in every confirmation and the
     "Manage" page, so nothing needs copying.
   - A progress timeline per student: notes, goals and scores over time.
   - A sibling booking ("book both kids back to back").
10. **Their words:** students; parents/guardians; families; sessions or lessons;
    subjects; packages; attendance; progress notes; terms.

**Recommended Tutor template:**
- **Home cards:** Today's sessions · Packages running low · New students ·
  Sessions this week · Unpaid.
- **Tabs:** Today, Calendar, Students, Openings, Notes (progress), Settings.
- **Intake:** grade/level; subject and goals; parent/guardian name, email and
  phone (required if under 18); online or in person; learning needs; plus the
  parent consent below.
- **Services:** Free assessment 30 min; Math 60 min $60; Reading & Writing 60 min
  $55; SAT/ACT prep 90 min $80; package of 10.
- **Rules:** a weekly standing session; packages; a 24 h cancellation window with
  makeup credit.
- **Follow-ups:** a progress note to the parent after each session.
- **Hide:** My look, aftercare, portfolio, birthday, deposits (on by choice).
  **Show:** standing appointments, intake, packages, online link.

**Pitch:** "Weekly sessions, packages and parent updates that send themselves. $25
flat, with no per-lesson fees as you grow."

## 4. Car rental (independent, 1-20 cars) *(later; not in this build)*

**The tools:** Booqable (general rental, cheapest), HQ Rental Software, Rent Centric,
TSD Rental, RENTALL, RentWorks, Rentgine, CarCEO, Turo (a marketplace where many
small owners start).

1. **Must-haves:**
   - **Bookings by the day:** pickup and return times; online reservations;
     a list of vehicles and their availability.
   - **The renter:** a driver's licence scan (front, back and a selfie); a
     rental agreement signed online.
   - **The car:** photo inspections at pickup and return; mileage and fuel
     recorded at both.
   - **Money:** a security deposit hold; extras (child seat, delivery).
2. **Dashboard:** today's pickups and returns; cars out and cars available;
   vehicles due for service; claims in progress; revenue per car.
3. **Booking rules:**
   - **Timing:** by the day or the hour; a buffer between rentals for cleaning;
     a late-return grace period (for example 30 min), then an hourly or daily
     fee.
   - **Renter limits:** a minimum age (often 21 or 25) and how long they've held
     a licence.
   - **Limits on use:** a daily mileage cap with a per-mile overage charge;
     return with the same fuel level.
4. **Intake:**
   - a licence check (real verification costs about $2 per check, as a
     third-party add-on)
   - proof of insurance or a protection plan
   - a signed agreement
   - photos of the car, before and after
5. **Customer page:** choose a car and dates; upload a licence; sign; pay a
   deposit; see pickup instructions; extend the trip.
6. **Money:**
   - **Up front:** a security deposit hold, released after a few days.
   - **After the trip:** fuel and cleaning fees; late fees ($20/hour or
     $25/day examples); mileage overage; damage charges.
   - **Owners:** owner payouts, for fleets.
7. **Top complaints:**
   - Turo hosts: its commission rose from 25% to 35% for the same protection;
     liability cover was cut; strict 24-hour windows to report damage; claims
     reversed months later; time lost on disputes.
   - Rental software: enterprise prices ($120-399/month) and slow set-up.
8. **Pricing:**
   - Booqable: about $29-149.
   - HQ: about $120-399.
   - Rent Centric: quote only, about $99+.
   - Turo: 15-40% of every trip.
   - **What Clientele could offer:** your own booking site with no commission,
     for $25-49/month.
9. **Wow features we could offer:**
   - Guided photo check-in and check-out: walk around the car, with timestamped
     photos the renter co-signs.
   - A deposit hold that releases itself.
   - A licence and insurance check before the keys are handed over.
   - "Book direct": move Turo regulars to your own page.
   - Per-car profit (revenue minus costs).
10. **Their words:** renters/guests; trips/rentals; vehicles/fleet; pickup/return;
    check-in/check-out; deposit; damage claim; mileage.

**Template notes for later:** `bookingUnit: "day"`, a vehicles list as
"resources", check-in/out inspections, deposit holds, a rental agreement with
licence and insurance. The template system already leaves room for these.

**Pitch:** "Your own rental site with no 35% commission: renters book, upload a
licence, sign and pay a deposit online, and you get photo check-in and check-out."

## 5. Hair stylist (checking what we already have)

**The tools:** GlossGenius, Vagaro, Square Appointments, Fresha, StyleSeat, Booksy.

1. **Must-haves:** a booking link; reminders; deposits/no-show fees; client
   notes and history; forms and waivers; payments; marketing texts. Clientele
   has all of these except built-in card checkout and marketing texts.
2. **Dashboard:** today's list; revenue; rebooking; new clients.
3. **Booking rules:** services from 30 min to 6+ hours (color, braids); deposits
   are common; consultations for big changes; patch tests for color.
4. **Intake:** allergies; patch test (48 h before color); hair history (relaxer,
   color, box dye); goals and inspiration photos; a chemical-service waiver;
   photo consent.
5. **Client page:** book without an account (GlossGenius stresses this; we do it
   too); rebook; see past visits.
6. **Money:** deposits; card on file; tips; cancellation fees; retail products.
7. **Top complaints:**
   - Marketplace commissions: StyleSeat about 30% of new marketplace clients,
     plus about $2.35 per booking charged to the client.
   - Fresha's switch from free to a 20% new-client fee.
   - GlossGenius's 2.6% card fee on top of its plan.
   - Vagaro's add-ons.
   - Forms that need work (GlossGenius users).
8. **Pricing:**
   - GlossGenius: $24-50+.
   - Vagaro: $30 + $10 per calendar.
   - Square: free, or $49/$149.
   - Fresha: free + 20% of new-client first bookings.
   - StyleSeat: commissions.
   - **Clientele:** $25 flat.
9. **What we already do better:** Regulars-only times; a waitlist that offers
   cancellations; client home with Pro ID; My look; aftercare emails; the
   Assistant. **Gaps:** color formula records; patch-test tracking; inspiration
   photo upload when booking; card on file for no-show fees.
10. **Their words:** clients; appointments; services; Regulars; consultation;
    patch test; formula.

**Current template:** keep it. Add a "Color formula" note field and a patch-test
question (yes/no, with date) to the starter intake.
