# Founding pros: outreach kit

Goal: 3–5 real pros (ideally one stylist, one barber, one tattoo artist, one
tutor) using Clientele for two weeks and telling you what's missing.
Everything below is copy you can paste and edit. Swap [brackets].

---

## 1. Who to ask first

In this order, because they're the most likely to say yes and be honest:
1. Pros you already know or have booked with.
2. Pros a friend books with (ask the friend to introduce you).
3. Local pros on Instagram with 500–5,000 followers who take bookings by DM
   or "text to book" in their bio. They feel the pain most.

Skip big salons and shops for now: one independent pro with their own book is
the customer.

---

## 2. The founding-pro offer

> **Founding pro:** free for 2 months (normally $25/month), I set you up
> myself, and you tell me what to fix.

- Keep it to 5–10 people so it stays personal.
- The free months: once they sign up, send Claude their business name. Their
  free trial is stretched to 2 months from signup. After that they see the
  normal "choose your plan" prompt and pay $25/month (or $250/year) if they
  stay. No card is needed until then.

---

## 3. First messages (DM or text)

Short, personal, one question at the end. Send from your own account.

**Hair stylist**
> Hi [name], love your silk presses! I'm building Clientele, a booking app
> for independent stylists: clients book and pay a deposit from one link, get
> reminders, and you see your week and what you've earned at a glance. I'm
> looking for 5 founding stylists to use it free for 2 months and tell me
> what to fix. Would you be up for a 10-minute look?

**Barber**
> Hey [name], your fades are clean. I'm building Clientele, a booking app for
> independent barbers: one link for your bio, reminders so fewer no-shows,
> and a waitlist that fills cancellations for you. Looking for a few founding
> barbers to use it free for 2 months and tell me what's missing. Want to
> take a look?

**Tattoo artist**
> Hi [name], really like your fine-line work. I'm building Clientele for
> independent tattoo artists: clients send their idea, placement and
> reference photos first, you approve before they book, deposits go straight
> to you, and healing check-ins go out on their own. Looking for a few
> founding artists to use it free for 2 months and tell me what to fix. Open
> to a quick look?

**Tutor**
> Hi [name], I'm building Clientele, a booking app for independent tutors:
> students (or parents) book from one link, you can send session notes to
> parents after each lesson, and session packs count down on their own. I'm
> looking for a few founding tutors to use it free for 2 months and give me
> honest feedback. Would you be up for trying it?

**If they say "what is it exactly?"**
> It's your booking page plus the business side: clients book and pay
> deposits from one link, get reminders and can reschedule themselves, and
> your Home shows how full your week is, what you've earned this month, and
> one-tap moves to fill gaps (like reminding someone who owes you or inviting
> back a regular who's overdue). Try the live demo: www.clientelesuite.com

**Follow-up (3–4 days later, once only)**
> Hey [name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure at all.
> Happy to set it up for you in 10 minutes if you're curious.

## 4. When they say yes: the 10-minute setup

Do it with them on a call or in person the first time. Send this too:

> Here's the 10-minute setup:
> 1. Sign up at **www.clientelesuite.com/signup** and pick your trade. You'll
>    get an email to confirm.
> 2. Read and sign the short agreement.
> 3. **Services:** tap "Use these services" to start from examples, then
>    change the names, prices and times to yours.
> 4. **Hours:** set the days and hours you work.
> 5. **Openings:** open the times clients can book (this is the step people
>    miss; clients only see times you open).
> 6. **Share:** Home → "Share booking link". Put it in your bio and text it
>    to 3 regulars.
> 7. Optional: Settings → Deposits → Connect Stripe to take deposits.
>
> Tell me the moment anything is confusing. That's exactly what I need.

After they sign up: check **Admin → Launch week** daily. If they're stuck
for 2 days, tap "Email a nudge" (or text them).

---

## 5. Week-one check-in (day 7)

Ask on a call if you can; otherwise send these. Write down their exact words.

1. Did any client book through your link? How did they find it?
2. What was the most confusing or slowest part of setting up?
3. Is there anything you still do outside Clientele (DMs, Notes app, a paper
   book, Venmo requests)? What is it?
4. What would make you pay $25 a month for this without thinking about it?
5. What's one thing you'd change tomorrow if you could?
6. Would you tell another [stylist / barber / artist / tutor] about it? Why
   or why not?

Bring the answers back here and we'll decide the next build from them.

---

## 6. Day 14: the ask

> You've had Clientele for two weeks. Would you keep using it? Anything that
> would stop you? And would you mind if I quoted you (first name and city)
> on the site?

Their quotes replace the placeholder testimonials on the landing page.
