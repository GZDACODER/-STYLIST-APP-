# Clientele on Instagram: profile setup and 2-week posting plan

Everything referenced here is in this folder:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `profile-picture.png` | Profile picture (gold ring, script C) |
| `highlights/*.png` | 5 highlight covers: start, how, pros, founding, faq |
| `reel-booking.mp4` | Reel: a client books in seconds (live booking page) |
| `reel-home.mp4` | Reel: your week at a glance (live Home demo) |
| `posts/dayNN-*-N.png` | Every post image. `-1`, `-2`… are carousel slides, in order |

All post images are 1080×1350 (portrait, the size Instagram shows biggest in the feed). Reels and stories are 1080×1920.

---

## 1. Profile setup (10 minutes)

**Username:** `@clientelesuite` (already yours).

**Name** (this is searchable, so put the key words in it):
```
Clientele | Booking for Pros
```

**Category:** Software (or App Page). Turn **Display category** on.

**Bio** (fits Instagram's 150 characters):
```
Booking app for stylists, barbers, tattoo artists & tutors ✂️
One link. Deposits. Fewer no-shows.
Founding pros: 2 months free ↓
```

**Link:** `https://www.clientelesuite.com`

**Contact options:** Email → `hello@clientelesuite.com`. Leave phone and address off.

**Profile picture:** upload `profile-picture.png`.

**Highlights.** Make these after your first stories (a highlight needs at least one story). Set each cover from `highlights/`:

| Highlight | Cover | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| Start here | `start.png` | What Clientele is (Day 1 story), the founding-pro offer |
| How it works | `how.png` | Both Reels, shared to your story |
| For pros | `pros.png` | Barber, tattoo and tutor posts, shared to your story |
| Founding | `founding.png` | `stories/story-link.png` with a link sticker |
| FAQ | `faq.png` | Your answers from the Questions sticker (Day 13) |

**Instagram's "Set up your professional account" steps** (the 1 of 5 screen):
- **Introduce yourself:** do this by posting Day 1 (below). It ticks this step.
- **Tell us your goals:** pick *Get more website visits* (or *Grow followers*).
- **Grow your audience:** do this *after* Day 3, so friends who follow see 2–3 posts, not an empty page.
- **Explore tools and insights:** skip for now. Look at Insights after week 2.

**Pin 3 posts** (tap ••• on a post → Pin to your profile) once they're up: Day 1 (Meet Clientele), Day 2 (booking Reel), Day 12 (Founding pros).

## 2. The 2-week plan

Post at about **11 AM** or **7 PM** Central (pros check their phones between clients and after work).
Each day, also share the post to your story.

**Hashtags.** Use 5–8, different mixes, at the end of the caption:
- General: `#hairstylist #barber #tattooartist #tutor #smallbusiness #bookingapp #salonowner #beautybusiness`
- Chicago: `#chicagohairstylist #chicagobarber #chicagotattoo #chicagostylist #chicagosmallbusiness`
- Trade-specific: `#silkpress #knotlessbraids #fades #barberlife #tattooapprentice #onlinetutoring`

## 3. Every day (10 minutes)

- **Reply to every comment and DM** within a few hours. Early on, this matters more than posting.
- **Comment on 5–10 local pros' posts** (real comments about their work, not "check out my app"). This is how they find you.
- **Share each new post to your story.** On Day 1, also post `stories/story-link.png` with a **Link** sticker to clientelesuite.com, and save it to the *Founding* highlight.
- When someone DMs "founding": send them the one-pager for their trade (`docs/outreach/`), then make them a founding pro in **Admin → Pros** once they sign up.

## 4. After 2 weeks

Check **Insights** (Professional dashboard): which posts got the most *saves* and *profile visits*. Do more of those. When 2–3 founding pros are using Clientele, ask each for one sentence about it. Those become your first ads.
